# Meteo 0.9.4

- Add a complete ICM profile with refreshed temperature and cloud-top colors,
  alongside the unchanged Classic ICM and Quick forecast presets.
- Redesign chart legends with miniature curves, ranges, bars and wind arrows,
  aligned series controls and responsive layouts. Explain precipitation types
  and storm intensity, and show pressure and wind color scales with values and
  units. Preserve custom colors, light and dark themes, layer toggles and PNG export.
- Align profile chart controls on narrow screens, with a consistent menu for
  moving and removing charts instead of per-row action overflow.
- Collapse hidden menu entries and separators, removing empty areas from mobile
  overflow menus and profile actions.
- Preserve intentionally empty legacy profiles and transparent ARGB colors
  when editing, importing and exporting series appearances.
- Keep secondary-axis tick positions consistent with their own configured
  ranges, including linked axes with alternate units.
- Apply angular breaks to filled wind-direction areas as well as their outlines.
- Validate range partners by weather quantity, unit and role. Reject edits that
  target profiles or charts which have already been deleted.
- Include request duration when calculating HTTP cache freshness, and distinguish
  fresh document reads from offline fallback reads.
- Prevent queued image callbacks from accessing destroyed responses after map
  loading is cancelled.
- Simplify preference rules and expand regression coverage for chart preparation,
  CPU/GPU parity, responsive legends and Material controls.
