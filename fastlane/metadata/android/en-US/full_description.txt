Explore ICM UW weather forecasts from meteo.pl in a native, open source application.

• Detailed meteograms: temperature ranges, precipitation, humidity, pressure, wind direction, clouds, visibility and fog
• Hourly and daily forecasts
• UM4 forecasts for Poland and Central Europe, and global GFS forecasts
• Custom chart profiles, mixed series, styles and configurable axes
• Pinch to zoom and swipe through the forecast
• Weather maps with color legends and preloaded animation
• Place search, saved locations and optional system location access
• PNG chart exports and PNG/CSV parameter readings
• Synoptic commentary and articles
• Data caching for faster loading and access to previously downloaded forecasts

Meteo contains no advertisements, tracking, account requirements or API keys. Location access is optional. Coordinates are sent to the weather and geocoding services when those features are used.

This is an independent application. Forecasts and articles are provided by ICM UW/meteo.pl; their content is subject to the providers' terms and is not covered by the application's GPL license.
